## Executive observations

### EU business AI-use share changed +6.47 pp versus 2024

Eurostat reports 19.95% in 2025 versus 13.48% in 2024, an increase of 6.47 percentage points. This is adoption, not realized ROI.

Source: [Eurostat official enterprise AI adoption](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/databrowser/view/isoc_eb_ai/default/table)

### Surveyed organizations' reported AI use changed +10.00 pp versus 2024

The OWID/McKinsey all-geographies survey reports 88.00% in 2025 versus 78.00% in 2024. It covers use in at least one function and is not directly comparable with Eurostat.

Source: [OWID / McKinsey / Stanford enterprise AI adoption](https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/share-companies-using-artificial-intelligence)

### Regional survey spread: 9.0 pp in 2025

Within the same OWID/McKinsey organization-use survey, Europe records 91.0% and Asia-Pacific records 82.0%, a 9.0 percentage-point observed range. Regional definitions overlap and sampling/weighting differ; this does not establish causality or representative country-level demand.

Source: [OWID / McKinsey / Stanford enterprise AI adoption](https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/share-companies-using-artificial-intelligence)

### 317 indexed links; 207 from core publisher feeds

## What cannot yet be concluded

- **Verified AI-specific revenue:** No audited AI-only revenue series is connected. Cloud segment revenue is not equivalent to AI revenue.
- **Representative enterprise AI payments:** Ramp and private purchase/payment data remain licensed or sample-limited.
- **Completed agent actions and transactions:** No comparable transaction/fulfillment outcomes dataset is connected.
- **AI infrastructure return on invested capital:** Even with company-wide audited PPE cash spending, AI-specific expenditure, leasing, contribution profits and segment revenue are not isolated.
